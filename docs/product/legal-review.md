# Legal review pack

**Prepared for the owner's lawyer. Not legal advice, and no statement here says that anything is lawful.**

This pack describes what Ledgerly collects, why, where it's stored, who sees it, how long it's kept, what users can do about it, and the questions we need answered before launch ([#54](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/54), a launch blocker in [roadmap M4](../planning/roadmap.md#m4-hardening--saas-launch)). It holds facts and questions only. Each fact links to the page or code it comes from, so it can be checked. Where our own docs and code disagree, section 10 lists both sides and doesn't pick one.

- **As of:** `main` at `adbe091`, 2026-10-20, with what's live updated for Deploy run 174 on 2026-10-21 ([#506](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/506)). That run deployed `main` at `9415606`, everything merged before it included, and each merge to `main` since deploys too ([environments and deploys](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#cicd-github-actions)), #486's build (`c9a7499`) and #503's (`e8fbfec`) among them. **Two things still wait on the owner:** the one-off backfills (step 5's receipt clean-up deletes nothing until one has run, and another marks the placeholders left before [#342](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/342)), and Resend's sending domain ([#3](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/3)). Until the domain is verified, the server's emails in section 4 aren't delivered; the owner's test shows whether it is.
- **It already has real users.** There is one Appwrite project, `ledgerly-dev`, and it is production ([environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments)). The app is still being built, so some rules are written down but not built yet. Those are marked **planned**.
- **What Ledgerly is:** an app that records gig work, shared house bills, personal bills and loans in one ledger. It **records** money; it never moves it. People pay each other outside the app ([vision](vision.md), [D-014](../architecture/decisions.md#d-014-we-record-money-we-dont-move-it)).
- **Who it's for:** owner to confirm (Australia only, or anyone who signs up). Question 11 asks what changes if people outside Australia sign up.
- **Words used here:** "the owner" is the person who owns Ledgerly the business and keeps its accounts, billing, secrets, console access and approval of production releases; the lead agent, an AI coding agent, runs the project day to day ([D-015](../architecture/decisions.md#d-015-the-lead-agent-runs-the-project-day-to-day)). "The group" means a group's joined members, people who moved out included. It doesn't include placeholders ([row permissions](../architecture/permissions.md#row-permissions-by-table)). A placeholder with no account can read nothing; one who has an account sees only the group's name, type and whether it's archived (and, for a pair group, the other member's name) until they join ([D-016](../architecture/decisions.md#d-016-being-added-to-a-group-doesnt-make-you-a-member-you-join)).

## 1. The data we hold

Account deletion is live, with a delete screen (since Deploy run 174, top). It doesn't delete everything a user is in. It deletes their own account rows. A group is deleted too, with everything in it, only when nobody else is in its team: this pack calls that a **purged** group. The last person in an archived group can also delete the group itself ([#429](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/429)), which purges it the same way, under the same rule ([deleting a group](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#deleting-a-group)). Placeholders and people who left on their own aren't in a team, so a personal group, and a pair or employer group whose other side is a placeholder, is purged. A group someone else is still in is **kept**, and the user's records in it stay, under "Former member". A group they never joined, where they're still a placeholder, is left exactly as it is: their placeholder row, under the name others typed, and any invite to it, address included, stay ([each group they joined](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#each-group-they-joined)). The last column says which happens. The full rules: [deleting an account](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#deleting-an-account-m4).

## 2. Placeholders: people others typed in

A **placeholder** is someone a user added to a group, by name and maybe an email or phone, who hasn't joined it. Usually they have no account, and they never agreed to anything. Someone who has an account stays a placeholder in that group until they join it ([D-016](../architecture/decisions.md#d-016-being-added-to-a-group-doesnt-make-you-a-member-you-join)).

- **What's stored:** the typed name, and **one** typed key: the email if one was given, else the phone ([adding someone](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#adding-someone-to-a-group)). Placeholders added before #252 can have both.
- **Who sees it:** members see only the name the group uses. Contact details are never shown to other members ([privacy rules](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#privacy-rules)).
- **What they receive:** nothing automatic. They can receive what a user sends on purpose: an invite by email (a house sends one from its housemates list, and a pair from its Settings: [#412](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/pull/412), [#417](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/pull/417)), a "you owe me" reminder, or an invoice to an employer's billing email (section 4).
- **How they take over their records:** by verifying the typed email and signing in, or by accepting an invite, on the page its link opens ([claim](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#claim-linking-records-to-a-new-account)). Someone who signs up for it takes the placeholder's records over directly. Someone who already has an account takes them over by a **merge** ([merging](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#merging-two-persons), [claim.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/adbe091848a3f275ec3151d11283402445564a58/functions/identity/src/routes/claim.ts)): it joins them to the placeholder's groups and moves its records to them. In a group they left on their own, or a pair or employer group they're already in, the placeholder keeps its row and its shares there, with no keys left. In any group the account hasn't joined, the placeholder's records stay on the placeholder, so the merge doesn't show the group who the placeholder became ([merging](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#merging-two-persons)). A merge cut off partway carries on at their next sign-in, or within about an hour, when `identity`'s hourly run finishes it ([finishing merges](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#finishing-merges)). A placeholder added by phone can't be claimed yet, since phone verification comes after v1 ([F-05](features.md#accounts-and-profile)).
- **Removal:** only a house can remove a member: an admin removes them while nothing there names them. Employer and pair groups refuse it ([delete-member.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/adbe091848a3f275ec3151d11283402445564a58/functions/groups/src/routes/delete-member.ts)), so an employer, or the other side of a pair, stays until the group is purged. 30 days after their last member row goes, step 5c deletes their identities and the invites to them and renames them "Former member". Finding 3.

## 5. What users can do

- **See and download their data:** the Export page builds CSV files in the browser: a group's records, the user's own records across groups, and their profile, which includes their email, phone and bank details ([data export](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#data-export)). It doesn't include receipt files, statement PDFs, the activity feed, transactions' notes, line items, void reasons or due dates, schedules, the user's other verified emails, invites, reminders, or Appwrite's session records (IP, device, country) ([export-data.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/adbe091848a3f275ec3151d11283402445564a58/apps/web/src/features/export/export-data.ts)), and it doesn't include groups the user left on their own ([what's kept](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#what-is-kept-and-why)).
- **Correct it:** the user edits their name, settings and business details on Profile ([profile route](../architecture/functions-api.md#identity)). The name a group uses for them is set by that group's admins. A record's creator or an admin can edit or void it, except in a closed period ([roles](../architecture/permissions.md#roles)). They change the email they sign in with on Profile, with their password; the new address must be verified, and their old verified addresses are told ([change notice](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#email-and-phone-change-notice)). There's no screen yet to change the phone. A forgotten password is reset from **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, by an emailed link ([password recovery](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-recovery)).
- **Delete their account** ([#50](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/50)), from a delete screen that links to the export first: see [deleting an account](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#deleting-an-account-m4). It deletes their account, identities, profile, invites and reminders (section 1). It keeps each group someone else is still in, where the person becomes "Former member" and their final statements stay as issued, ABN and bank details included ([D-010](../architecture/decisions.md#d-010-closed-periods-never-change)). A group nobody else is in is deleted with everything in it, invoices to an employer who never joined included. A group they never joined is left as it is, their placeholder row and any invite to it included. In a kept group, the schedules they made or that name them end; in a kept pair or employer group, every schedule made before the deletion ends. After that, nobody can name them in a new record in a group they had joined, except a payment or an adjustment, so balances can be settled ([deleted people](../architecture/functions-api.md#ledger)).
- **Delete a group** ([#429](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/429)): the last person in an archived group can delete it if they're its admin, or whatever their role once it has no joined admin (for an employer group whose worker deleted their account, the employer; live, [#486](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/486)), with everything in it, final statements and invoices included, from a screen that links to the export first, as account deletion does. A group anyone else is still in can't be deleted ([deleting a group](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#deleting-a-group)).
- **Placeholders:** section 2.

## 7. Security summary

- **Only the server writes to the database.** Every change goes through a server function that checks who's asking and their role ([D-003](../architecture/decisions.md#d-003-clients-never-write-to-the-database), [function-side checks](../architecture/permissions.md#function-side-checks)). The app uploads receipt files to Storage itself, and manages the user's own password and sessions with Appwrite.
- **Records are visible to the group only** (a receipt not yet attached aside: section 1), and members' contact details to the server only ([row permissions](../architecture/permissions.md#row-permissions-by-table)). An employer group's billing emails are the exception: the group can read them.
- **Claiming records needs a verified email or phone** ([only verified keys move](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#only-verified-keys-move)). Only verified users can upload files ([storage](../architecture/permissions.md#storage)).
- **Logs hold no names, emails, phones or amounts;** they hold user, person and row IDs, which whoever runs the project can link to a person ([personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data)). An ID is checked only for its shape, and a client chooses some IDs: Appwrite lets it choose its own user ID at sign-up (the app doesn't), and the API takes a client's ID for a group, a transaction, a schedule, a reminder and a receipt file. So someone calling the API directly could choose one that looks like a name.
- **Deleting an account** needs a sign-in from the last 10 minutes and no password set, changed or reset in the last 24 hours (on an account without a password, an email or phone change counts too) ([refusals](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#refusals)). Deleting a group needs the same ([#429](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/429)). Setting, changing or resetting a password emails the account ([password notice](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-notice)). A change of email emails the account's old verified addresses, and a change of phone its verified email ([change notice](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#email-and-phone-change-notice)).
- **Other sessions:** Profile's **Sign out other devices** signs out every session but the one in use. A password reset signs out no session by itself; signing in after it signs out the others, which stay signed in until then ([password recovery](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-recovery)).
- **Rate limits:** reminders, 1 per sender per group in 3 days, 3 per recipient in 7 days and 20 per sender in 24 hours; invites, 3 per address in 7 days and 20 per inviter in 24 hours. Both are counted under a guard, so requests sent together can't all pass ([threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls)). Since [#274](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/274), every address the sender types for either is counted before it's checked, 10 in 24 hours, and only a new invite or reminder that's sent gives its count back (a reminder's retry never does), so every other check adds up: wrong addresses, retries, and refusals or failed sends after the check.
- The full threat list and the secrets inventory: [security and privacy](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md).

## 10. Findings: where our docs and code disagree

These are for the lead to settle. The pack doesn't take a side.

1. **Backups and the Appwrite plan.** [Backups](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#backups) and [environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) say production needs backups, which Appwrite offers on Pro and Enterprise only. [Function settings](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#function-settings) says the organisation is on the GitHub Student Pack plan. Whether that plan has backups is [restore step 0](../engineering/restore.md#0-the-plan-has-backups), for the owner, and launch waits on it ([#567](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/567)). [Retention](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#retention) depends on the answer.
2. **Appwrite's sign-in emails through Resend.** [D-011](../architecture/decisions.md#d-011-resend-for-all-outgoing-email), [outside services](../architecture/tech-stack.md#outside-services) and [environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) say Appwrite sends through Resend. That's a console setting, and the repo's [appwrite.config.json](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/adbe091848a3f275ec3151d11283402445564a58/appwrite.config.json) can't show it.
3. **Removing a placeholder.** [Personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data) says to delete a placeholder's details on request: an admin removes the placeholder from each group, and 30 days after its last member row goes, step 5c deletes its identities and the invites to them and renames it "Former member" ([#342](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/342); one removed before #342 waits for the one-off backfill, top). But only a house can remove a member: employer and pair groups refuse it ([delete-member.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/adbe091848a3f275ec3151d11283402445564a58/functions/groups/src/routes/delete-member.ts), [functions API](../architecture/functions-api.md#groups)), so an employer, or the other side of a pair, keeps their name and typed address until the group is purged, which the same page says only further on. There's also no route, form or contact address for the request itself, so a placeholder, who has no account, has no way to ask, and a house that doesn't remove them, or can't while a record there names them, keeps their name and typed address.
4. **Membership privacy on prod.** [Threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls) says the setting must be set on prod when prod is created. [Environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) says `ledgerly-dev` is production, with no separate prod.
