# Legal review pack

**Prepared for the owner's lawyer. Not legal advice, and no statement here says that anything is lawful.**

This pack describes what Ledgerly collects, why, where it's stored, who sees it, how long it's kept, what users can do about it, and the questions we need answered before launch ([#54](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/54), a launch blocker in [roadmap M4](../planning/roadmap.md#m4-hardening--saas-launch)). It holds facts and questions only. Each fact links to the page or code it comes from, so it can be checked. Where our own docs and code disagree, section 10 lists both sides and doesn't pick one.

- **As of:** `main` at `4a8b0c4`, 2026-10-20. Deploys are paused until GitHub Actions runs again (the lead, on [#96](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/96)), so what's live is behind `main`. Which version is live: owner to confirm.
- **It already has real users.** There is one Appwrite project, `ledgerly-dev`, and it is production ([environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments)). The app is still being built, so some rules are written down but not built yet. Those are marked **planned**.
- **What Ledgerly is:** an app that records gig work, shared house bills, personal bills and loans in one ledger. It **records** money; it never moves it. People pay each other outside the app ([vision](vision.md), [D-014](../architecture/decisions.md#d-014-we-record-money-we-dont-move-it)).
- **Who it's for:** owner to confirm (Australia only, or anyone who signs up). Question 11 asks what changes if people outside Australia sign up.
- **Words used here:** "the owner" is the person who owns Ledgerly the business and keeps its accounts, billing, secrets, console access and approval of production releases; the lead agent, an AI coding agent, runs the project day to day ([D-015](../architecture/decisions.md#d-015-the-lead-agent-runs-the-project-day-to-day)). "The group" means a group's joined members, people who moved out included. It doesn't include placeholders ([row permissions](../architecture/permissions.md#row-permissions-by-table)). A placeholder with no account can read nothing; one who has an account sees only the group's name, type and whether it's archived (and, for a pair group, the other member's name) until they join ([D-016](../architecture/decisions.md#d-016-being-added-to-a-group-doesnt-make-you-a-member-you-join)).

## 1. The data we hold

Account deletion is built, with a delete screen, but not live: it reaches users once deploys resume (top). It doesn't delete everything a user is in. It deletes their own account rows. A group is deleted too, with everything in it, only when nobody else is in its team: this pack calls that a **purged** group. Placeholders and people who left on their own aren't in a team, so a personal group, and a pair or employer group whose other side is a placeholder, is purged. A group someone else is still in is **kept**, and the user's records in it stay, under "Former member". A group they never joined, where they're still a placeholder, is left exactly as it is: their placeholder row, under the name others typed, and any invite to it, address included, stay ([each group they joined](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#each-group-they-joined)). The last column says which happens. The full rules: [deleting an account](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#deleting-an-account-m4).

## 2. Placeholders: people others typed in

A **placeholder** is someone a user added to a group, by name and maybe an email or phone, who hasn't joined it. Usually they have no account, and they never agreed to anything. Someone who has an account stays a placeholder in that group until they join it ([D-016](../architecture/decisions.md#d-016-being-added-to-a-group-doesnt-make-you-a-member-you-join)).

- **What's stored:** the typed name, and **one** typed key: the email if one was given, else the phone ([adding someone](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#adding-someone-to-a-group)). Placeholders added before #252 can have both.
- **Who sees it:** members see only the name the group uses. Contact details are never shown to other members ([privacy rules](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#privacy-rules)).
- **What they receive:** nothing automatic. They can receive what a user sends on purpose: an invite by email (no screen sends one yet: finding 2), a "you owe me" reminder, or an invoice to an employer's billing email (section 4).
- **How they take over their records:** by verifying the typed email and signing in, or by accepting an invite once there's a page for it (finding 2) ([claim](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#claim-linking-records-to-a-new-account)). Someone who signs up for it takes the placeholder's records over directly. Someone who already has an account takes them over by a **merge** ([merging](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#merging-two-persons), [claim.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/functions/identity/src/routes/claim.ts)): it joins them to the placeholder's groups and moves its records to them. In a group they left on their own, or a pair or employer group they're already in, the placeholder keeps its row and its shares there, with no keys left. A merge cut off partway carries on at their next sign-in, or within about an hour, when `identity`'s hourly run finishes it ([finishing merges](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#finishing-merges)). A placeholder added by phone can't be claimed yet, since phone verification comes in M3 ([F-05](features.md#accounts-and-profile)).
- **Removal:** finding 6.

## 5. What users can do

- **See and download their data:** the Export page builds CSV files in the browser: a group's records, the user's own records across groups, and their profile, which includes their email, phone and bank details ([data export](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#data-export)). It doesn't include receipt files, statement PDFs, the activity feed, transactions' notes, line items, void reasons or due dates, schedules, the user's other verified emails, invites, reminders, or Appwrite's session records (IP, device, country) ([export-data.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/apps/web/src/features/export/export-data.ts)), and it doesn't include groups the user left on their own ([what's kept](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#what-is-kept-and-why)).
- **Correct it:** the user edits their name, settings and business details on Profile ([profile route](../architecture/functions-api.md#identity)). The name a group uses for them is set by that group's admins. A record's creator or an admin can edit or void it, except in a closed period ([roles](../architecture/permissions.md#roles)). There's no screen yet to change the account's email or phone. A forgotten password is reset from **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, by an emailed link ([password recovery](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-recovery)).
- **Delete their account** (built, not live until deploys resume; [#50](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/50)), from a delete screen that links to the export first: see [deleting an account](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#deleting-an-account-m4). It deletes their account, identities, profile, invites and reminders (section 1). It keeps each group someone else is still in, where the person becomes "Former member" and their final statements stay as issued, ABN and bank details included ([D-010](../architecture/decisions.md#d-010-closed-periods-never-change)). A group nobody else is in is deleted with everything in it, invoices to an employer who never joined included. A group they never joined is left as it is, their placeholder row and any invite to it included. In a kept group, the schedules they made or that name them end; in a kept pair or employer group, every schedule made before the deletion ends. After that, nobody can name them in a new record in a group they had joined, except a payment or an adjustment, so balances can be settled ([deleted people](../architecture/functions-api.md#ledger)).
- **Placeholders:** section 2.

| Browser history | A password reset link, with the user ID and the link's secret in it. The page takes it out of the address bar and Back/Forward, but the browser's own history can keep it ([password recovery](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-recovery)) | By the browser. The link works once, for 1 hour |
| Downloads | Files the user chooses to save: export CSVs (the profile file includes bank details), and receipt and statement PDFs ([data export](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#data-export)) | By the user; the app can't |
| The offline cache | The app's own code and icons; no records ([vite.config.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/apps/web/vite.config.ts)) | When the app updates |
| Memory | The records the user is viewing ([use-log-out.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/apps/web/src/features/auth/use-log-out.ts)) | On sign-out |

The app's own code sets no cookies; Appwrite sets its session cookie. There's **no analytics, no tracking and no advertising** in the web app. We searched for Google, analytics, Segment, PostHog, Sentry, Plausible, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Hotjar, Datadog, font CDNs and any outside URL. The Appwrite SDK includes an optional tracking module, which the app doesn't use ([lib/appwrite.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/apps/web/src/lib/appwrite.ts)).

## 7. Security summary

- **Only the server writes to the database.** Every change goes through a server function that checks who's asking and their role ([D-003](../architecture/decisions.md#d-003-clients-never-write-to-the-database), [function-side checks](../architecture/permissions.md#function-side-checks)). The app uploads receipt files to Storage itself, and manages the user's own password and sessions with Appwrite.
- **Records are visible to the group only** (a receipt not yet attached aside: section 1), and members' contact details to the server only ([row permissions](../architecture/permissions.md#row-permissions-by-table)). An employer group's billing emails are the exception: the group can read them.
- **Claiming records needs a verified email or phone** ([only verified keys move](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#only-verified-keys-move)). Only verified users can upload files ([storage](../architecture/permissions.md#storage)).
- **Logs hold no names, emails, phones or amounts;** they hold user, person and row IDs, which whoever runs the project can link to a person ([personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data)). An ID is checked only for its shape, and Appwrite lets a client choose its own user ID at sign-up (the app doesn't), so someone calling the API directly could choose one that looks like a name.
- **Deleting an account** will need a sign-in from the last 10 minutes and no password set, changed or reset in the last 24 hours ([refusals](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#refusals); built, not live until deploys resume). Setting, changing or resetting a password emails the account ([password notice](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-notice)). A change of email emails the account's old verified addresses, and a change of phone its verified email ([change notice](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#email-and-phone-change-notice)).
- **Other sessions:** Profile's **Sign out other devices** signs out every session but the one in use. A password reset signs out no session by itself; signing in after it signs out the others, which stay signed in until then ([password recovery](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-recovery)).
- **Rate limits:** reminders, 1 per sender per group in 3 days, 3 per recipient in 7 days and 20 per sender in 24 hours; invites, 3 per address in 7 days and 20 per inviter in 24 hours. Both are counted under a guard, so requests sent together can't all pass ([threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls)). **Planned** ([#274](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/274)): wrong addresses typed for either count against the sender, 10 in 24 hours.
- The full threat list and the secrets inventory: [security and privacy](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md).

## 10. Findings: where our docs and code disagree

These are for the lead to settle. The pack doesn't take a side.

1. **Receipt clean-up.** [Personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data) says a receipt taken off its last transaction is deleted about 24 hours later, and [step 5](../architecture/scheduling.md#step-5-receipt-clean-up) describes how. On `main` the scheduler has no step 5 ([run.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/functions/scheduler/src/routes/run.ts)), so no receipt file is deleted yet ([#163](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/163)).
2. **Invites.** [Invites](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#invites), the [functions API](../architecture/functions-api.md#identity) and the [invite rate limits](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls) describe them. On `main`, `identity` has `POST /invites` and `POST /invites/accept`, by email only ([main.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/functions/identity/src/main.ts)). The docs describe an `/invite#<token>` page ([invites](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#invites), step 5) and an invite step in [user flow 6](user-flows.md#6-create-a-house-and-add-a-bill). The web app has no screen that sends one and no page for the emailed link, so the link opens the home page and does nothing ([routes.tsx](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/apps/web/src/routes.tsx)). The accept page is **planned** ([#195](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/pull/195)).
3. **Backups and the Appwrite plan.** [Backups](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#backups) says production uses Appwrite's managed backups (Pro plan). [Environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) says Pro is the owner's call, and [function settings](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#function-settings) says the organisation is on the GitHub Student Pack plan. [Retention](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#retention) depends on backups.
4. **Sending invoices automatically.** [F-29](features.md#employer) lists "optional auto-send". [Drafting](../architecture/statements.md#1-draft-statements-job-hourly) says the job finalizes without sending, and the code agrees ([draft-due.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/functions/statements/src/routes/draft-due.ts)).
5. **Appwrite's sign-in emails through Resend.** [D-011](../architecture/decisions.md#d-011-resend-for-all-outgoing-email), [outside services](../architecture/tech-stack.md#outside-services) and [environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) say Appwrite sends through Resend. That's a console setting, and the repo's [appwrite.config.json](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/appwrite.config.json) can't show it.
6. **Removing a placeholder.** [Personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data) says to delete a placeholder's details on request. There's no route, form or contact address for that request, and removing a placeholder's member row leaves their name and typed key in `persons` and `identities`; [#342](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/342) plans deleting the identities and replacing the name 30 days after a placeholder's last member row goes (scheduler step 5c; not at once, so a removal can't be used to test an address) ([delete-member.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/functions/groups/src/routes/delete-member.ts)).
7. **The invited address.** An invite link expires after 14 days ([threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls)), but no rule deletes the invite row, which holds the invited address, except account deletion: the inviter's, the invitee's in a group they joined, and a purge of the group ([order](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#order)). [Step 5b](../architecture/scheduling.md#step-5b-invite-clean-up) and [retention](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#retention) now say a row is deleted 30 days after it's accepted or expires, but the scheduler doesn't do it yet ([#343](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/343)).
8. **Bill reminders.** [Step 3](../architecture/scheduling.md#step-3-reminders), [F-52](features.md#personal) and [user flow 10](user-flows.md#10-personal-rent-with-a-reminder) describe a "bill due soon" email. The scheduler doesn't send one yet ([reminders.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/4a8b0c42133af0ae173aace821ade6bb4c4f048c/functions/scheduler/src/steps/reminders.ts), [#33](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/33)).
9. **Membership privacy on prod.** [Threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls) says the setting must be set on prod when prod is created. [Environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) says `ledgerly-dev` is production, with no separate prod.
