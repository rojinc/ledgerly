# Legal review pack

**Prepared for the owner's lawyer. Not legal advice, and no statement here says that anything is lawful.**

This pack describes what Ledgerly collects, why, where it's stored, who sees it, how long it's kept, what users can do about it, and the questions we need answered before launch ([#54](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/54), a launch blocker in [roadmap M4](../planning/roadmap.md#m4-hardening--saas-launch)). It holds facts and questions only. Each fact links to the page or code it comes from, so it can be checked. Where our own docs and code disagree, section 10 lists both sides and doesn't pick one.

- **As of:** `main` at `6fd8081`, 2026-10-19.
- **It already has real users.** There is one Appwrite project, `ledgerly-dev`, and it is production ([environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments)). The app is still being built, so some rules are written down but not built yet. Those are marked **planned**.
- **What Ledgerly is:** an app that records gig work, shared house bills, personal bills and loans in one ledger. It **records** money; it never moves it. People pay each other outside the app ([vision](vision.md), [D-014](../architecture/decisions.md#d-014-we-record-money-we-dont-move-it)).
- **Who it's for:** owner to confirm (Australia only, or anyone who signs up). Question 11 asks what changes if people outside Australia sign up.
- **Words used here:** "the owner" is the person who owns and runs Ledgerly the business ([D-015](../architecture/decisions.md#d-015-the-lead-agent-runs-the-project-day-to-day)). "The group" means a group's joined members, people who moved out included. It doesn't include placeholders, who can read nothing ([row permissions](../architecture/permissions.md#row-permissions-by-table)).

## 2. Placeholders: people others typed in

A **placeholder** is someone a user added to a group by name, and maybe an email or phone, who hasn't signed up. They never agreed to anything.

- **What's stored:** the typed name, and **one** typed key: the email if one was given, else the phone ([adding someone](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#adding-someone-to-a-group)). Placeholders added before #252 can have both.
- **Who sees it:** members see only the name the group uses. Contact details are never shown to other members ([privacy rules](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#privacy-rules)).
- **What they receive:** nothing automatic. They can receive what a user sends on purpose: an invite (**planned**), a "you owe me" reminder, or an invoice to an employer's billing email (section 4).
- **How they take over their records:** by verifying the typed email and signing in ([claim](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#claim-linking-records-to-a-new-account)). Someone who signs up for it takes the placeholder's records over directly. Someone who already has an account takes them over by a **merge** ([merging](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#merging-two-persons), [claim.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/identity/src/routes/claim.ts)): it joins them to the placeholder's groups and moves its records to them. In a group they left on their own, or a pair or employer group they're already in, the placeholder keeps its row and its shares there, with no keys left. A merge cut off partway carries on at their next sign-in; an hourly job to finish it is **planned** (finding 13). A placeholder added by phone can't be claimed yet, since phone verification comes in M3 ([F-05](features.md#accounts-and-profile)).
- **Removal:** finding 7.

## 5. What users can do

- **See and download their data:** the Export page builds CSV files in the browser: a group's records, the user's own records across groups, and their profile, which includes their email, phone and bank details ([data export](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#data-export)). It doesn't include receipt files, statement PDFs or the activity feed, and it doesn't include groups the user left on their own ([what's kept](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#what-is-kept-and-why)).
- **Correct it:** the user edits their name, settings and business details on Profile ([profile route](../architecture/functions-api.md#identity)). The name a group uses for them is set by that group's admins. A record's creator or an admin can edit or void it, except in a closed period ([roles](../architecture/permissions.md#roles)). There's no screen yet to change the account's email or phone. A forgotten password is reset from **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, by an emailed link ([password recovery](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-recovery)).
- **Delete their account** (**planned**, [#50](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/50), finding 1): see [deleting an account](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#deleting-an-account-m4). It keeps final statements as issued, ABN and bank details included ([D-010](../architecture/decisions.md#d-010-closed-periods-never-change)), and it keeps groups others still use, where the person becomes "Former member". After that, nobody can name them in a new expense, bill or loan in a group they had joined; payments and adjustments still can, so balances can be settled ([deleted people](../architecture/functions-api.md#ledger)).
- **Placeholders:** section 2.

## 7. Security summary

- **Only the server writes to the database.** Every change goes through a server function that checks who's asking and their role ([D-003](../architecture/decisions.md#d-003-clients-never-write-to-the-database), [function-side checks](../architecture/permissions.md#function-side-checks)). The app uploads receipt files to Storage itself, and manages the user's own password and sessions with Appwrite.
- **Records are visible to the group only,** and contact details to the server only ([row permissions](../architecture/permissions.md#row-permissions-by-table)).
- **Claiming records needs a verified email or phone** ([only verified keys move](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#only-verified-keys-move)). Only verified users can upload files ([storage](../architecture/permissions.md#storage)).
- **No personal data in logs** ([personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data)).
- **Deleting an account** will need a sign-in from the last 10 minutes and no password set, changed or reset in the last 24 hours (**planned**, [refusals](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#refusals); coded but not deployed, finding 1). Setting, changing or resetting a password emails the account ([password notice](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-notice)). A change of email or phone is to email the account too (**planned**, finding 12).
- **Other sessions:** Profile's **Sign out other devices** signs out every session but the one in use. A password reset signs out no session by itself; signing in after it signs out the others, which stay signed in until then ([password recovery](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#password-recovery)).
- **Rate limits** on reminders, and on invites (**planned**) ([threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls)).
- The full threat list and the secrets inventory: [security and privacy](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md).

## 10. Findings: where our docs and code disagree

These are for the lead to settle. The pack doesn't take a side.

1. **Account deletion.** [Personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data) and [retention](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#retention) describe it as working, and say the delete screen tells users to export first. On `main`, the `account` function has a `POST /delete` route with the refusals ([delete-account.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/account/src/routes/delete-account.ts)), but it isn't in [functions.json](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/appwrite/functions.json), so it isn't deployed, and there's no delete screen. It doesn't yet purge a group nobody else is in, and it waits on that before deleting identities, the profile and the Appwrite user. Every user has a personal group, so for now it would delete none of those for anyone. [#50](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/50) is building the rest.
2. **Receipt clean-up.** [Personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data) says a receipt taken off its last transaction is deleted about 24 hours later, and [step 5](../architecture/scheduling.md#step-5-receipt-clean-up) describes how. On `main` the scheduler has no step 5 ([run.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/scheduler/src/routes/run.ts)), so no receipt file is deleted yet ([#163](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/163)).
3. **Invites.** [Invites](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#invites), the [functions API](../architecture/functions-api.md#identity) and the [invite rate limits](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls) describe them. On `main` the `identity` function has no invite route ([main.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/identity/src/main.ts)).
4. **Backups and the Appwrite plan.** [Backups](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#backups) says production uses Appwrite's managed backups (Pro plan). [Environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) says Pro is the owner's call, and [function settings](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#function-settings) says the organisation is on the GitHub Student Pack plan. [Retention](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#retention) depends on backups.
5. **Sending invoices automatically.** [F-29](features.md#employer) lists "optional auto-send". [Drafting](../architecture/statements.md#1-draft-statements-job-hourly) says the job finalizes without sending, and the code agrees ([draft-due.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/statements/src/routes/draft-due.ts)).
6. **Appwrite's sign-in emails through Resend.** [D-011](../architecture/decisions.md#d-011-resend-for-all-outgoing-email), [outside services](../architecture/tech-stack.md#outside-services) and [environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) say Appwrite sends through Resend. That's a console setting, and the repo's [appwrite.config.json](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/appwrite.config.json) can't show it.
7. **Removing a placeholder.** [Personal data](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#personal-data) says to delete a placeholder's details on request. There's no route, form or contact address for that request, and removing a placeholder's member row leaves their name and typed key in `persons` and `identities` ([delete-member.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/groups/src/routes/delete-member.ts)).
8. **The invited address.** An invite link expires after 14 days ([threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls)), but no rule deletes the invite row, which holds the invited address, except account deletion: the inviter's, or the invitee's in a group they joined ([order](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#order)). Both are **planned**.
9. **Bill reminders.** [Step 3](../architecture/scheduling.md#step-3-reminders), [F-52](features.md#personal) and [user flow 10](user-flows.md#10-personal-rent-with-a-reminder) describe a "bill due soon" email. The scheduler doesn't send one yet ([reminders.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/scheduler/src/steps/reminders.ts), [#33](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/33)).
10. **Membership privacy on prod.** [Threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls) says the setting must be set on prod when prod is created. [Environments](../engineering/environments-and-deploys.md#environments) says `ledgerly-dev` is production, with no separate prod.
11. **D-017.** #287's spec lists D-017 to read, but [decisions](../architecture/decisions.md) has no D-017; it goes from D-016 to D-019.
12. **The email and phone change notice.** [Threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls) describes it as working, and as covering a first password set through an email or phone change. On `main` it's written down ([change notice](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#email-and-phone-change-notice)) but not built: there's no template or route for it, and `identity` subscribes only to the password events ([functions.json](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/appwrite/functions.json)). Its build is [#268](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/issues/268).
13. **Parts of a merge the docs describe and `main` doesn't do yet.** [Activity](../architecture/data-model.md#activity) and [threats and controls](../architecture/security-and-privacy.md#threats-and-controls) say a merge moves an activity row's subject from the placeholder to the account; the merge on `main` doesn't touch activity rows ([merge.ts](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/functions/identity/src/merge.ts)). [Finishing merges](../architecture/identity-and-invites.md#finishing-merges) describes an hourly job that finishes a merge without waiting for a sign-in; `identity` has no schedule ([functions.json](https://github.com/rojinc/ledgerly/blob/main/appwrite/functions.json)).
